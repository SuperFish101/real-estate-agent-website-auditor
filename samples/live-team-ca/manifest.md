## Why this page's exposure is different from the other live run

**This page shows photographs of sold properties.** The other live California run in `output/`
shows a portrait and decoration and no property at all. AB 723 is about images of real property,
so a page carrying listing photography is the case the statute was written for. That raises the
value of the answer, and it does not change the verdict: the answer is not known, so the section
is INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE and every row says what would settle it.

Note also that the six sales shown are described as sold. Whether AB 723 reaches an image of a
property that is no longer being advertised for sale is a question of law, not a question about
this artifact, and this auditor does not answer questions of law. It is named here so a person
can take it to a lawyer rather than assume either way.

## What would settle it

One sentence per image from whoever made the site, saying what was done to it. The wording rules
are in `templates/manifest.md`. Naming any element added, removed or changed makes the image
covered under `CA-10140.8(b)(1)`. Naming only lighting, colour, angle or cropping makes it
excluded under `CA-10140.8(b)(2)`.

## What this manifest does NOT let the auditor conclude

That the images are fine. An absent fact is not a passing fact. No image on this page has been
cleared, and the run says so in those words.
