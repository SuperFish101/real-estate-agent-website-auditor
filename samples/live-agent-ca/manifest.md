## What would settle it

One sentence per image from whoever made the site, saying what was done to it. The wording
rules are in `templates/manifest.md`. Naming any element added, removed or changed makes the
image covered under `CA-10140.8(b)(1)`. Naming only lighting, colour, angle or cropping makes
it excluded under `CA-10140.8(b)(2)`.

## What this manifest does NOT let the auditor conclude

That the images are fine. An absent fact is not a passing fact. No image on this page has been
cleared, and the run says so in those words.
